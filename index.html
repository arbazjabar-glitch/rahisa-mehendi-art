Secret to Deep Color

4 Steps to a Royal Mahogany Stain

Because our paste is 100% natural and free of chemical dyes, follow these steps to achieve maximum color depth:

1

Keep On for 6-8 Hours

Allow the paste to dry naturally. Dab our lemon-sugar sealant spray once semi-dry to keep the paste adhering to the skin.

2

Scrape, Never Wash!

Gently scrape off dry henna using a butter knife or card edge. Strictly avoid water or soap for the first 12 hours.

3

Apply Rahisa Aftercare Balm

Rub our herbal aftercare balm or Eucalyptus oil over palms. The natural heat stimulates deeper penetration of Lawsone dye.

4

Peak Stain in 48 Hours

Initial pumpkin-orange color oxidizes into a rich, dark mahogany/burgundy stain within 48 hours just in time for your big day!

📊 Direct Google Sheets Webhook Integration

Every time a customer places an order or books a bridal appointment, the website can automatically append a new row into your private Google Sheet in real time!

📋 Quick 30-Second Google Sheets Setup Guide:
  1. Open a new Google Sheet and name the first tab Orders.
  2. In Google Sheets menu, click Extensions > Apps Script.
  3. Paste this simple code snippet into the editor: 
    function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    new Date(),
    data.type || 'Order',
    data.id || '',
    data.name || '',
    data.phone || '',
    data.items || data.package || '',
    data.total || 0,
    data.address || data.city || '',
    data.notes || ''
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput("Success").setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.TEXT);
}
  4. Click Deploy > New deployment, select Web app, set Who has access to Anyone, and click Deploy.
  5. Copy the resulting Web App URL and paste it into the input above!
